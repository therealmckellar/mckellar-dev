Cannabis, liquor, and hospitality are three industries with massive operational complexity and zero tolerance for errors. Compliance requirements change by state. Order volumes spike unpredictably. Margin pressure is constant. And yet, most operators in these industries still run on manual processes, phone calls, and spreadsheets.

That's where AI agents change the game — not by replacing people, but by automating the compliance, order management, and communication workflows that eat up hours every day.

## Cannabis: Compliance Without the Headcount

Cannabis operators deal with one of the most complex regulatory environments in business. Every state has different rules for tracking, reporting, and compliance. A single missed filing can cost hundreds of thousands in fines.

**Where AI agents help:**

- **Metrc/Salesforce compliance agent** — monitors inventory movements, flags discrepancies before they become violations, auto-generates state reports
- **License renewal agent** — tracks renewal dates across multiple licenses and jurisdictions, alerts on deadlines, generates required documentation
- **Cultivation data agent** — pulls environmental and growth data, generates compliance reports, flags anomalies

**The impact:** Compliance tasks that take 10-15 hours/week per location drop to 2-3 hours of review. The agent does the heavy lifting; the compliance officer reviews and signs off.

## Liquor: Order Management at Scale

Liquor distributors deal with thousands of SKUs, complex pricing tiers, and seasonal demand swings. Order processing is manual, error-prone, and slow.

**Where AI agents help:**

- **Order intake agent** — parses orders from email, phone, and POS systems. Extracts line items, validates pricing, checks inventory, creates fulfillment records
- **Pricing intelligence agent** — monitors competitor pricing, suggests price adjustments based on market conditions, flags margin compression
- **Replenishment agent** — tracks inventory levels across locations, auto-generates purchase orders based on sales velocity and seasonal patterns

**The impact:** Order processing time drops from 15 minutes per order to 2 minutes of review. Errors decrease by 80% because the agent validates every line item.

## Hospitality: Guest Experience Automation

Hotels, restaurants, and event venues deal with high guest expectations, thin margins, and seasonal volume. Most operations run on a patchwork of PMS systems, reservation platforms, and manual communication.

**Where AI agents help:**

- **Reservation management agent** — handles bookings across platforms, optimizes table/room allocation, manages waitlists and cancellations
- **Guest communication agent** — sends pre-arrival information, handles routine requests (directions, parking, dietary restrictions), escalates complex needs to staff
- **Event coordination agent** — manages event timelines, vendor communication, setup/teardown schedules, and post-event follow-up
- **Review management agent** — monitors review platforms, generates response drafts, flags negative reviews for immediate attention

**The impact:** Staff spend less time on phone calls and emails, more time on guest experience. Revenue per available seat/room increases because optimization improves occupancy and upsell timing.

## The Common Thread

In all three industries, the pattern is the same:

1. **Manual workflows eat hours every day** — data entry, compliance checks, order processing, communication
2. **AI agents automate the triage phase** — agents handle the repetitive work, humans make decisions
3. **The stack stays the same** — agents integrate with existing POS, PMS, ERP, and CRM systems
4. **ROI is measurable** — hours saved, errors reduced, revenue gained
